If you're looking to predict the correct final result of a football game, you're playing in the 1x2 market.</p></div><div class="mb-1"><h2 class="h6 text-secondary">How do Sporticos 1x2 Predictions Work?</h2><p class="text-secondary">Our handy graphics show you quickly and easily which of the three is the most likely outcome in the soccer game of your choosing. If for example you see a "1" next to Arsenal vs Liverpool, that means Arsenal are the likely winners of that contest.
